**Search-intent split.** "Mixed-use" searches come from two audiences. One is the construction side (GC or developer before the building exists). The other is the operations side (property manager after turnover). The reference pages only speak to operations ("24/7, call now"). This page should open on the construction side and close on operations. That is the differentiator found in the competitor audit: same firm, from rough-in to 24/7 service.

## 4. Conversion strategy

1. **Two CTAs, two audiences.** Primary: **"Request a Bid"** (GC/developer, form anchor). Secondary: **"Call Local Now"** (PM/owner, `tel:5036422067`). The reference pages only use "Schedule Commercial Service", which doesn't fit someone sending plans.
2. **Form.** Add "Mixed Use" to the Industry dropdown (missing today on this page). Recommend two optional fields: *Project stage* (Pre-construction / Under construction / Occupied building) and *Plans or bid documents* (upload or link). **[CLIENT VALIDATION REQUIRED]** — needs a form change by the web team.
3. **Tracking.** Fire a separate GA4/Ads conversion event per CTA (form submit vs. phone click) so bid requests and service calls are not mixed in reporting.
4. **Fix live phone bugs before sending traffic:** `tel:5038776971e` (stray "e") and the 503-642-2067 link that dials the Salem number on the reference pages.
